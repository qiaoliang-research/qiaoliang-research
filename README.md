## Research

### AuthorityLens

**Human-centered control for delegated AI agents**

AuthorityLens is a usable-security / HCI research prototype investigating what can still happen after a user revokes an AI agent's access.

It focuses on cases where a local permission may show `OFF` while system-level consequences remain, including:

- residual delegated authority,
- pending effects,
- unresolved external state.

The project includes an executable authority model, an interactive prototype, a deterministic benchmark with 40 synthetic scenarios, and a pilot-ready human-comprehension study design.

> **Research question:**  
> After a user tells an autonomous system to stop, what can still happen — and how should the system help them know?

### Moral Consistency Variance

**Decision stability under moral prompt perturbations in large language models**

My sole-authored, peer-reviewed paper studies whether language models maintain stable decision distributions when the underlying moral dilemma remains fixed but prompt framing changes.

The study uses **KL divergence, Jensen–Shannon divergence, and decision flip rate** across 50 synthetic moral dilemmas.
